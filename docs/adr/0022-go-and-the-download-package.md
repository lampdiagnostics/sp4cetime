# ADR-0022: ▶ Go, the Download package, ▷ Paths and the Capture tiles

Status: accepted (4 November 2026, 0.4.3).

## Context

The owner set out the whole Starsigns video in one sequence: press ▶ Go; from the calendar, fade into Fly mode and fly straight to the first view (above Earth, facing the Sun); a clock caption appears bottom right, starting at the moment given in the dialog and ticking on as if you had travelled back to it; the sweep round the planets; a gentle landing in the field at the place, where a place caption joins the clock, held 5 seconds; the field (its glowing grid) dropping away from your feet while the sky tilts to the final wheel; the wheel held 5 seconds; the recording stops and a Download package pops up. Separate ▶ Go and ◉ Go + Record buttons, ✶ Create Path and ⤓ Download package in the dialog were too many choices.

## Decision

1. **One ▶ Go** in the Starsigns dialog and in **▷ Paths** (the renamed ▷ Present): it saves the path to the list, flies it and records it from the first frame (the fade into Fly mode included) as the MP4 made for X (`size: 'small'`, ADR-0020). At the last point's end the recorder hands back its current frame (`Recording.still()`, a JPEG of the recording canvas, and whether its sides are dark), the recording stops, the presentation ends **keeping the final view**, and the **Download package** pops up (`src/app/PackageDialog.tsx`, loaded when first needed).
2. **The Download package:** ⤓ Download (free) saves one .zip: the MP4, the PDF and the path token. ✶ Customize and ☕ Buy me a coffee lead to the shop (`SHOP_URL`, sp4cetime.store; the coffee at `/coffee`), where the paid options and Stripe live. sp4cetime.org shows no prices (ADR-0018).
3. **The PDF is the video's last frame** (`starsignsFramePdf`, `src/core/starsignsPdf.ts`): one page the frame's own shape, 11 in wide, the JPEG filling it (`PdfPage.image`, DCTDecode, written byte for byte). When the path ends on a Starsigns chart, the reading (from the last point's chart and moment) is set on the sides around the central circle: left, the moment and place and every body's sign and house by name ("house 5 · Pleasure"); right, the twelve houses by name with the sign each begins in, the Ascendant and Midheaven, "positions are astronomy; houses are astrology". One text size for both columns, the largest that fits; the left column starts below the frame's date box, the right stops above its captions. Light text on a dark frame, dark on a light one (the Bright look). Without a chart, the frame alone. The 5 × 7 in card (`starsignsCard`) stays for the shop.
4. **Time runs from the moment.** Starsigns points are `playing` at speed 1, and carry `zone` (the place's offset from UTC, minutes; new optional field of `PathPoint` and of the file, rule 16). A flight to a point less than an hour from the clock, with time running there too, carries the clock on (`flights.ts`): no hold, no jump back. The first point (a jump of decades) sets the clock to the moment; it runs from the arrival.
5. **Captions**, bottom right above the scale bar, mono, 26 px (about 20 pt), off-white, in captures: the clock `1985 Jul 14   23:30:00` (in the point's zone, or the device's own) whenever a path is presented or a chart is on; the place `Winnipeg   Lat 49-52-59 N   Lon 97-09-58 W` from the landing on. Both go with all text (the space bar's second press).
6. **The drop-away.** From the ground view to the wheel (a turn on the spot) the camera takes 6 s (`DROP_SECONDS`); over the same 6 s the field falls 2 m · (e^1.15t − 1) km (a metre in half a second, 20 m by two, 200 m by four, 2 km by six), fading over its last 1.4 s; the planisphere lens opens over 4 s. The ground view and the wheel are each held 5 s; the second wheel point (the title) is gone.
7. **▷ Paths** has the list, ▶ Go, ✎ Continue, ⤓ Download, Delete and a **drop box** for path tokens (drag a .sp4ce.json onto it, or click to choose); an uploaded path is selected, ready to go. Shift still plays a path without recording. A token needs nothing else: each point holds the camera (anchor body, frame, position in km, orientation, field of view, up), the moment and how time runs, the look, the pause, the sound and the zone.
8. **◉ Capture as six tiles** (2 × 3): rows small (for X) and large (hi-res), columns picture (.png: fitted to 1920 × 1080, or the screen's full resolution), GIF (600 or 1000 px, 5 s) and video (.mp4: 1080p made for X, or up to 4K). At least 72 px tall, for a thumb. Keys P, G, V take the large ones.

## Consequences

- The dialog lost ⤓ Download package, the video-size choice, ✶ Create Path and the coffee link; they live in the package now.
- A path stopped early (■ Stop, Esc) has no package; its video still finishes and goes to Downloads only through Capture.
- Paths saved before 0.4.3 have no `zone` (the clock shows the device's time) and Starsigns ones hold time still between points (they were made with `playing: false`); make the path again for the new tour.
